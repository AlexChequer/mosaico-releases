# The Mosaico name and icon

Mosaico's code is free software under the GPL-3.0 (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)): you can use it, study it, change it and share your changes, as that license says.

**The license covers the code, not the name or the icon.** The name "Mosaico" and the app icon (`Sources/Mosaico/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset`, `docs/images/icon.png` and the sources in `design/icone/`) identify this project, so people know which app they are getting and who stands behind it.

## What's fine

- Building Mosaico from this repository for yourself, unchanged or with your own changes, and keeping its name and icon on your own Mac.
- Saying that your project is "based on Mosaico", "a fork of Mosaico" or "works with Mosaico".
- Writing about Mosaico, with its name and icon, in articles, reviews, videos and talks.
- Packaging an unchanged release of Mosaico (Homebrew, for example) under its name.

## What needs another name and icon

If you distribute a modified version (a fork), give it **its own name and its own icon**, not "Mosaico" or something that could be confused with it, and don't present it as the official app. Keep the copyright notices and the GPL-3.0, as the license requires.

## Questions

Not sure whether something is fine? Open an issue and ask.
